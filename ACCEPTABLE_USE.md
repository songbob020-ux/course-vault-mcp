# Acceptable use

Use this software only for sources you are authorized to access and in accordance
with applicable terms and law. It must not be used to bypass authentication, DRM,
paywalls, access controls, rate limits, or anti-bot systems; share credentials;
download protected videos; or redistribute course transcripts and media.

The browser-side collector should read only content normally exposed to the
authenticated user. The durable output should be the user's own summaries,
source locators, hashes, and review conclusions.
